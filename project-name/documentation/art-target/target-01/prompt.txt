Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: visual target for genuine Blender geometry in a Babylon Lite 3D multiplayer dungeon game.
Primary request: One cohesive original low-poly miniature dungeon concept plate, inspired by the gameplay archetypes of classic Gauntlet, without copying existing game assets.
Composition: landscape concept sheet. Upper two-thirds: steep top-down orthographic cutaway dungeon encounter, graphite beveled modular stone tiles and waist-height walls, warm brass torches, teal spectral altar, four clearly separated playable heroes, scattered treasure, large gold key, food, and a teal exit arch. Lower third: clean isolated full-body miniatures showing four heroes and four enemy archetypes, all uncropped and spaced apart on charcoal background. No writing or labels.
Heroes: broad red-armored Warrior with oversized axe; gold-armored Valkyrie with winged helmet, round shield and sword; purple-robed Wizard with pointed hat and staff; slim green-hooded Elf holding curved bow. Compact toy-like proportions, large readable equipment silhouettes, simple faces. In scene give the heroes separate cyan, magenta, yellow and lime thin ground rings to denote player identity independent of class.
Enemies: floating pale-teal ghost with tapering ragged body; stocky ochre grunt with club; horned dark-red demon; hunched brown lobber carrying a large spherical bomb. These are original designs, readable geometric miniatures.
Style: actual buildable-looking low-poly meshes, flat matte material families, bevel highlights, no detailed textures. Dark stone remains brighter than black, heroes have distinct value and silhouette, sparse emissive glow. Warm/cool light balance. Whole scene visible, not cinematic blur.
Constraints: prioritize readability from above; no logos, no text, no UI screenshot, no photorealism, no gore, no billboard/card imagery. Concept input only, not an assertion of finished game or Blender render.
