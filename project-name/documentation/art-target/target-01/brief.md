# Gauntlet 3D art target 01

Task: gauntlet-3d-art. Worker: /root/visual_target. Status: ready as an art-direction target, not finished production art or a Blender render.

## Inspected observations
The generated 1536 x 1024 PNG contains a coherent cutaway stone dungeon and an uncropped lineup of four heroes and four enemies. Graphite masonry, warm orange torches, teal altar/exit, red warrior, gold valkyrie, purple wizard, and green elf meet the requested palette. The encounter shows cyan, magenta, yellow and lime ground rings independently of class colors. The four monsters have distinct ghost, grunt, demon and lobber silhouettes. Large key, treasure and food are visible. All eight lineup subjects are completely framed.

## Prioritized 3D actions
1. Preserve class silhouettes before facial detail: broad axe and shoulder blocks; winged helmet and disc shield; triangular pointed hat and flared robe; hood and unmistakably curved bow. Keep equipment thick enough to read at steep top-down camera angles.
2. Make enemy type readable by outline and value: floating taper ghost, squat club grunt, horned angular red demon, hunched bomb-bearing lobber.
3. Use broad beveled stone modules with brighter top surfaces; keep walls low or use camera obstruction treatment. Preserve clear navigable gaps and avoid dressing clutter in combat lanes.
4. Separate fixed class materials from bright player rings and overhead identity indicators. Same-class players must remain distinct through ring/indicator color.
5. Restrict emissive material to spectral entities, altar, exit and small magical accents. Torches add amber rhythm without overwhelming floor contrast.

## Editable assumptions and budget
Hero height approximately 1.6 world units, a head about 1/4 of height; final collision capsule and map grid determine scale. Hidden backs are inferred as simple continuation of costume, not image-proven geometry. Ground plinths in the lineup are presentation aids, not required geometry. Solid low-poly shapes, shared flat materials and small geometry budgets are sufficient: roughly 300–1500 triangles per hero, 200–1000 per enemy, simple repeating wall/floor geometry. These are direction budgets, not measured output counts.

## Conflicts and simplifications
The target includes decorative wings on the demon, stone cracks, bones, chests and small props beyond the minimum brief. These are optional, not requirements. Reduce cracks and tiny features; do not bake the full image or use billboard stand-ins. The scene camera is less steep than the intended playable top-down camera, so final silhouettes must be evaluated in the real camera. The image does not demonstrate duplicate-class players, multiplayer, animation, topology or export validity. The demon is disproportionately large; normalize collision/gameplay scale deliberately. No essential framing mismatch requires a regeneration.

## Comparison criteria
Use this frozen target version against a genuine Blender preview. Check silhouettes, bevel scale, material value contrast, warm/cool balance, and uncluttered traversal first. No genuine Blender preview was provided to this worker, so no 3D comparison has been claimed.
